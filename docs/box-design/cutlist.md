# Chota box: cut list for review

A counter-top wooden box for a Raspberry Pi 5 kitchen gadget. Outside: 424 W × 262 D × 180 H mm closed. Butt-jointed, glued and pinned/screwed, built by a hobbyist with a drill, a saw, a router (rebates) and clamps.

Layout: the left zone (244 mm wide) holds a recessed control deck (two dials, a needle meter, a 30 mm arcade button, toggles, LEDs) under a lid whose inner face is a 10.1" screen module (235 × 161, routed rebate, 4.5 mm lip). The lid is a 20 mm frame with a 6 mm ply skin, hinged on a fixed 42 mm hardwood strip along the back; it opens 110° and a brass chain stops it. Right zone: a printer bay (thermal receipt printer, listed 140 W × 180 D × 125 H, not measured yet; 40 mm behind it for plugs) under a service lid of the same construction, with the paper slot (90 × 5) in the service lid. Under the deck, front: two sealed speaker chambers (6 mm ply, the front wall as baffle) for 3.5" drivers; behind them the Pi 5, a 3-outlet powerboard with the Pi supply, the printer brick and the amp plug-pack, a USB sound card and a small amp. The mic board, a TALK button and a camera sit behind the front rim above the deck. One power cord.

Hardware: 2 pairs × Brass butt hinges, 40 mm (mortised into the hardwood back rails and hinge strip); 2 × Brass chain, about 150 mm (on screw eyes in hardwood; stops the lid 20° past upright); 1 sheet × Brass mesh (two speaker grilles and the mic grille; 3–4 mm clear of the cones, held so it can’t rattle); 1 × Brass slide for the camera shutter (in a routed groove over the camera window); 1 × Brass tear bar, 90 mm (along the slot’s front edge (from Paper Console)); 2 × Magnet pairs (lid catches); 2 × Dowels, 6 mm (seat the service lid so the slot lines up); 8 × M3 brass screws (deck down onto its cleats; it lifts out as one harness); 4 × Rubber feet, 8–10 mm (airflow under the box; 188–190 mm tall overall); 1 sheet × Closed-cell foam, 1–2 mm (gaskets: mic board, screen clips, driver frames); a handful × Polyfill (pillow stuffing) (loosely in each speaker box); 1 × PVA wood glue, finish oil (glue-up and finish).
