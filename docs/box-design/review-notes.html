What the two reviews changed (v2)

A Sonnet agent and Gemini each checked the list against the drawing. Both found real errors; the table above already includes these fixes.

  • The lid was too shallow. Screen plus speakers left no wood between them. The lid grew 12 mm for a cross-rail, so the box is 262 deep, not 250.
  • The lid came out 34 mm, not 28. The skin sat on top of 28 mm frame strips. The strips are now 22 mm.
  • The divider sat beside the lid joint, not under it. It moved 6 mm, so both lids rest on it.
  • Zero clearance. The lids, deck and skins are now 1 mm under their openings.
  • Hinges and chains screw into hardwood: a 20 mm back rail on each lid and a cleat under the hinge strip. Ply edge grain won't hold them.
  • Added: screen clips with foam, a ribbon notch through the hinge strip, the angled paper guide, magnet catches, high vents at the back and right end, and 8–10 mm rubber feet.

Still open:

  • 81 mm under the deck. A plug-pack standing in a powerboard is 85–100 mm tall. Use a powerboard with side-facing outlets, or swap the Pi's plug-pack for a cable supply.
  • Heat. The Pi, two supplies and an amp make about 12–18 W in a closed ply box. Run it for an hour with a thermometer inside before you trust it.
  • Measure your ply. "12 mm" sheet runs 11.5–12.2 mm. Cut the ends, divider and deck to the walls you actually built, not to this table.
  • Build order: measure the printer and parts; cut the vents and holes; glue the cleats on; glue the walls into a square ring; add the base. Make the hinge strip and lids last, sized from the finished box.
