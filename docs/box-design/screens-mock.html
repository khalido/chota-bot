Do the literary clock's quotes fit the 5"?

Yes, at 30 px or larger for almost every minute. I measured all 4,676 quotes from curios in Georgia, in a 736 × 390 px box on the 800 × 480 panel, assuming a 108 mm wide screen. Short quotes can run larger, as the one above does at 44 px.
